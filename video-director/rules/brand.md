# Brand

- **Match the live website.** Take colours, fonts, tagline, headings, the primary call to action and tone from the site's copy and CSS. Record them in the client's brand profile before designing the first frame (profiles live in the private brands folder; see SKILL.md). For a new client, copy `templates/BRAND.md`.
- **One accent colour, used sparingly:** on the key number, the proof label or a highlight ring. Everything else stays in the product's own palette.
- **Frame 0 is the finished title** (logo mark plus title), so the first frame and the thumbnail always carry the brand.
- **End card:** lockup, URL at 36 px or larger, and one call to action matching the site's primary button at 48 px or larger, held at least 2 s after everything settles. The wordmark is always whole.
- **Mockup first.** Any change to an approved look is shown as a one-frame before/after mockup and signed off before the film is rebuilt.
- **Precedence:** brand profile > house rules > HyperFrames defaults.
