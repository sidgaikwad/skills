# Interview tree

Walk the branches in order: later branches depend on earlier answers. Ask one question at a time, each with a recommended answer (from step 1's facts, the brand profile, or the house default given here). Look facts up instead of asking; put only decisions to the user. Write each settled answer into `video-plan/BRIEF.md` straight away. The bracket after each branch says where it goes.

When the user brings screenshots, first list what each one shows (screen, state, the terms on it) and map it to a story beat; then ask only about the gaps.

## 1. Purpose `[message, ## Intent]`

- What should a viewer do right after watching? Recommend the site's primary call to action.
- What is the one thing they must remember, in one sentence in the viewer's words? This becomes `message`.
- Why now: a launch, a feature release, sales enablement, an investor update?
- How will we know it worked: views, demo bookings, the sales team using it?

## 2. Audience `[audience, CONTEXT.md]`

- Who exactly is watching: role, company size, industry? What do they already know about the product?
- What words do they use for the problem? Record them in CONTEXT.md next to the product's own terms. Recommend the product's term wherever it matches the buyer's.
- Technical or not? Non-technical buyers get plain full sentences and no developer words.

## 3. Where it plays `[destination, aspect, length]`

- Which channels: YouTube, the LinkedIn or X feed, the website hero, sales emails, events? Recommend a 16:9 master plus a 1:1 or 4:5 feed cut.
- Sound on or off? Feeds autoplay muted (92% of US mobile viewers watch muted), so feed videos are text-led; YouTube and website viewers usually have sound on.
- Length. Recommend 45–75 s for a feature video, 15–30 s for a feed cut, and 15–20 s for a hero teaser.

## 4. The story spine `[angle, PLAN.md]`

- The customer: a named, fictional company or order from the buyer's world. Recommend one concrete order, for example "Cascade Bike Shop ordered 30 rear wheels."
- The problem, as a number that hurts: a late ship date, lost money, a failed audit.
- The cause the product reveals.
- The fix: one action in the product, shown continuously.
- The proof: the view that shows it fixed. Recommend the same view that showed the problem.
- What is deliberately left out? Every extra feature dilutes the one story.

## 5. Product access `[## Assets]`

- What can we record: the live app (local or staging) with demo data, existing recordings, or only screenshots or Figma?
- Demo data: fictional names, realistic numbers for the buyer, and a problem big enough to matter. Who seeds it?
- What must never appear: real customer names, prices, internal tools, developer chrome?

## 6. Words `[voice, ## Customizations]`

- Voiceover or text-led? Recommend text-led for feed videos, plus a voiceover version for YouTube or the website when the feature needs explaining. A voiceover means a professional human voice or top-tier text-to-speech.
- Tone, for example "a calm senior colleague, never a sales pitch." Lines that must be said? Words to avoid?
- Brand lines to reuse: the site's tagline and headings.

## 7. Look `[## Notes, mockup]`

- Is there an approved brand profile? Use it unchanged, or change something? Any change goes through the mockup gate.
- Reference videos they love (links), and what exactly they love in each.
- Stage (light or dark), accent colour and typography. Recommend taking all three from the live site.

## 8. Motion `[## Customizations]`

- Calm and editorial, or energetic? Recommend calm-confident: the house ease and one move per shot.
- One transition family for the whole film: which one?
- Cursor: the house oversized cursor, or none?

## 9. Music and sound `[PLAN.md ## Music]`

- Mood and tempo, for example warm ambient piano at 80–100 BPM, soft acoustic, or minimal modern electronic. No vocals.
- Source and licence owner: their licensed library (Artlist, Musicbed, Epidemic Sound), the HeyGen catalog via `/media-use`, or the motion library kit?
- UI sounds (clicks, whooshes, chimes): yes or no? Anything they dislike, such as boomy impacts?

## 10. Logistics `[## Notes]`

- The deadline. Who approves, and who can overrule (for example a CEO)? How many review rounds?
- Deliverables: master, cut-downs, SRT, thumbnail, YouTube title and description?
- Where do the files go when done?
